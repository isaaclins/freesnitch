FreeSnitch watches every connection your Mac makes, asks before a new app reaches out, and keeps every answer on your Mac. No account, no telemetry, and every line is open source.

FreeMIT licence. No account, no trial.
Alerts

Allow once, or for good.

When an app reaches somewhere new, FreeSnitch asks. Pick the host, the app or the port, and how long the answer lasts. The menu bar shows what is happening right now.

Rules

Rules you can read.

Every rule says which app, where, and whether it is in force where you are. Pick an app from the list or drop it on the page. Blocklists and DNS over HTTPS sit next to them.

Everything it does
Places

Stricter at the café.

Make a place for home, work or the café, and choose how strict FreeSnitch is there. It knows where you are from the network you join, without asking for your location.

It fails open.

If FreeSnitch stops answering, your internet keeps working. A broken firewall never becomes an outage.

Signed and notarized.

Built with a Developer ID and checked by Apple. Updates are verified with a signature before they install.

Nothing phones home.

No analytics, no account, no licence check. It only contacts the blocklists and DNS you pick, and its update feed.

Where it stops

Blocklists work on names

They don't stop apps that connect to a fixed address, or that look names up with their own encrypted DNS, like Chrome or Firefox with DoH on.

The map is an estimate

Locations come from offline IP data, not measurement.

Blocking is opt-in

Out of the box FreeSnitch only watches. Rules and blocklists start when you turn on Enforcement in Settings.
